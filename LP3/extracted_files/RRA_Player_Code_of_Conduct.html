PLAYER CODE OF CONDUCT


Welcome to the Rajasthan Royals Academy Melbourne Elite Program.

Being selected for this program means you have been identified as a player with the talent, attitude, and potential to develop within one of the world’s most respected cricket ecosystems. That’s something to be proud of.

With that opportunity comes responsibility. This Code of Conduct outlines what we expect from every player in the program — not as a list of rules to follow, but as a set of standards that define who we are and how we operate.

The Royals Way is built on courage over comfort, curiosity over ego, and joy over jargon. This Code is an extension of that philosophy. It exists to protect the culture of the program, the experience of every player, and the reputation of the Rajasthan Royals brand.

By completing your registration, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to abide by this Code.

1. Our Values — What We Stand For

Everything in this program connects back to the Royals Way. These are not slogans — they are the standards we hold ourselves to every session, every interaction, every day.

Talent First

We identify potential before it’s obvious and invest early. Your talent earned your place here. Your attitude will determine what you do with it. Selection and opportunity skew toward growth, not just pedigree.

Play the Future

We develop cricket thinkers — players who can adapt, innovate, and make decisions under pressure. We adopt useful technology and data to inform decisions. We train for the game as it’s evolving, not as it was.

Learn by Doing

Skills are forged in context — match simulations, scenarios, and role reversals — not in isolated drills removed from the reality of the game. Leadership is practised, not preached. Everyone feels the weight of responsibility.

Mistakes are expected and valued. The rhythm of our environment is Mistake → Lesson → Adjustment. If you’re not making mistakes, you’re not pushing yourself hard enough. What matters is what you learn and how quickly you adapt. We don’t punish errors of intent — we coach through them.

Play Bold

Courage is a trainable skill. We reward intent and intelligent risk. Growth happens at the edge of your ability, not in the middle of it. We expect you to embrace challenge, take risks in training, and be brave enough to fail in pursuit of improvement. We define “positive” by options created, not only outcomes achieved.

Joy, Curiosity & Lifelong Learning

Cricket is a game. Games should be fun. Joy is not a distraction from performance — it’s a driver of it. We expect you to bring energy, enthusiasm, and enjoyment to every session.

The best players are the best learners. Ask questions. Seek feedback. Be open to new ideas — even when they challenge what you think you already know. Curiosity accelerates mastery.

Holistic Development

We coach the person, not just the cricketer. Performance is the product of skills, fitness, mindset, recovery, and life — all of them, not just one. Your nutrition, sleep, mental skills, and character all matter. Development that ignores the person behind the player is incomplete.

Fairness, Respect & Inclusion

We play hard and fair — honesty is non-negotiable. Every player in this program gets dignity of attention — no favourites, no shortcuts. Diverse backgrounds and ideas make us better and braver.

2. Player Expectations

2.1 Training & Effort

Arrive on time, properly equipped, and physically ready to train. Every session has a purpose — respect that by giving full effort from the first drill to the last.

Prepare your own equipment before each session. Warm up when directed. Cool down properly. Take responsibility for your physical preparation — hydration, nutrition, sleep, and recovery are part of being an elite athlete.

If you are injured or unwell, inform your squad coach before the session. If you cannot attend, notify the Academy as early as possible.

2.2 Communication

Speak to your coaches with respect and honesty. If you don’t understand something, ask. If you disagree with something, say so — respectfully. If you’re struggling with something, tell someone.

The best development relationships are built on trust, and trust requires honest communication in both directions.

Respond to Academy communications (emails, messages, notifications) promptly and politely.

For any administrative queries, contact the Admin Team at Eliteprogram@rramelbourne.com.

2.3 Respect & Sportsmanship

Treat every person in this program — players, coaches, staff, parents, and visitors — with respect. No exceptions.

Sledging, intimidation, bullying, discrimination, or exclusion of any kind will not be tolerated. Competition is encouraged. Cruelty is not.

Respect the facility. Clean up after yourself. Return equipment to its proper place. Leave every space better than you found it.

Win with humility. Lose with grace. Compete with intensity. Always shake hands.

2.4 Safety

Follow all safety instructions from coaches and staff without exception. Wear appropriate protective equipment as directed — helmets, pads, gloves, and any other gear required for the activity.

Report any injury, illness, or safety concern to your coach immediately. Do not train through pain without informing someone.

Do not engage in any activity that puts yourself or others at risk of injury.

2.5 Social Media & Representation

As a member of the Rajasthan Royals Academy, you represent the brand at all times — online and offline.

Do not post content that is disrespectful, offensive, or that could bring the Academy or the Rajasthan Royals into disrepute. Any use of Rajasthan Royals branding, logos, or imagery requires prior approval from the Academy.

Be proud of what you’re part of. Share your journey positively. But think before you post.

3. Behaviour Framework

We believe in development, not punishment. The vast majority of players in this program will never need to engage with this framework. It exists for the small number of situations where expectations are not met, and it operates on a principle of fairness, transparency, and proportionality.


TIER 1 — INFORMAL CONVERSATION

Minor issues addressed privately by the squad coach or Head Coach. The goal is awareness and reset — not discipline.

Triggers

•  Low effort or disengagement during a session

•  Arriving late or unprepared

•  Minor disrespect to a peer or coach

•  Not following session instructions after being directed

•  Negative body language or attitude that affects the group

Consequences

•  Private conversation to reset expectations

•  Reminder of this Code of Conduct

•  Noted internally but no formal record


TIER 2 — FORMAL WARNING

Repeated minor issues or a single moderate incident requiring formal documentation.

Triggers

•  Repeated low effort or disengagement after a Tier 1 conversation

•  Disrespectful behaviour toward coaches, staff, or peers

•  Unsportsmanlike conduct during competitive blocks

•  Failure to follow safety instructions

•  Inappropriate social media activity related to the Academy

•  Repeated lateness or unnotified absences

Consequences

•  Formal meeting with Head Coach and/or Academy Director

•  Written warning issued and documented

•  Parent/guardian notified (for U18 players)

•  Clear behaviour expectations set with agreed timeframe

•  May include temporary modified participation


TIER 3 — SUSPENSION OR REMOVAL

Serious breaches or failure to improve after a formal warning.

Triggers

•  Bullying, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation

•  Violent or threatening behaviour

•  Deliberate equipment damage or facility misuse

•  Bringing prohibited substances to any Academy activity

•  Repeated Tier 2 issues without improvement

•  Any conduct that endangers the safety of others

•  Bringing the Rajasthan Royals brand into disrepute

Consequences

•  Immediate meeting with Head Coach and Academy Director

•  Temporary suspension (duration determined case-by-case)

•  Parent/guardian meeting required (for U18 players)

•  In serious cases: permanent removal from the program

•  No refund of program fees for removal due to conduct

•  Decision communicated in writing

4. Support and Wellbeing

This Code exists alongside our commitment to your holistic development. If you are experiencing challenges — whether related to cricket, school, personal life, or mental health — our coaching team is here to help. Speak to your squad coach or any member of the Academy leadership in confidence.

The Royals Way says: we coach the person, not just the cricketer. We mean it.
