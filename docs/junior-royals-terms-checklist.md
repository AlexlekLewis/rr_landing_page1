# Junior Royals: what the Terms & Conditions must cover

Started 9 Oct 2026, updated 12 Oct 2026. Alex asked that the cancellation fee "goes
in the T's and C's when we are done", and on 12 Oct that the Terms make it clear
that re-enrolment is automatic and that the first payment comes out on the day of
the first session of the term (rules 2 and 3). Every rule the page promises is listed here, so the Terms say
the same thing as the page. **A lawyer reads the final Terms before enrolment
opens** (auto-renewal, cancellation fee and refunds are consumer-law areas).
