Royals Way · India Tour 2026

Privacy Notice

Australian Privacy Principles · last updated 18 May 2026
This notice explains what personal information we collect for the India Tour 2026, why we collect it, who can see it, and how long we keep it. It applies to every traveller's intake form and every supporting document you send us. It is governed by the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APP).

1Who we are

The Rajasthan Royals Academy Melbourne (RRA Melbourne, an arm of Royals Melbourne) is the entity collecting your information. The Head Coach, Alex Lewis, is the contact for all privacy matters: alex@rrmelbourne.com.au.

2Why we are collecting this information

We need it for one purpose: to run the India Tour from 26 June to 7 July 2026 safely and legally. Specifically:

  • Passport details, family information and travel history are used to submit an Indian e-Tourist Visa application on each traveller's behalf.
  • Medical conditions, allergies, dietary needs and emergency contacts are used so chaperones can care for your child on tour and respond to a medical incident.
  • Address, mobile and email are used to communicate about the tour and to issue invoices/receipts.
  • Parent/guardian details for minors are required by the Indian visa authority as part of the notarised parental consent.

We will not use your information for any other purpose without first asking you.

3What we collect

The intake form collects: full name (as on passport), date of birth, place of birth, gender, religion, occupation, nationality, passport number and expiry, residential address, mobile number, email, parents' full names and nationalities, countries visited in the last 10 years, medical conditions, medications, allergies, blood group, dietary requirements, Medicare and private health fund details, two emergency contacts, and signed consents. For minors we also collect both parents' contact details and IDs. Uploaded passport scans and photos are also stored.

4Who can see your information

Inside RRA Melbourne, only authenticated admin users of the RRA dashboard can read the information. As of writing, that is four people: the Head Coach, the CEO of Royals Melbourne, and two operational admins. Access is enforced by row-level security in the database — no anonymous reads are possible.

We will share specific items, only as needed:

  • Passport details and family information — uploaded to the Indian government's e-Visa portal (indianvisaonline.gov.in) for each traveller's visa application.
  • Names, dates of birth, passport numbers and emergency contacts — provided to the airline at ticketing and to the travel insurance provider when the policy is issued.
  • Medical conditions, allergies and emergency contacts — shared with chaperones on tour and with treating medical staff if there is an incident in India.
  • Passport scans and photos — shared with the travel agent handling our flight booking and with the Indian e-Visa portal.

We will not share your information with anyone outside this list without your explicit, written consent.

5Where it is stored

Information is stored in our Supabase database. Uploaded passport scans and photos sit in a private Supabase Storage bucket. Both are encrypted at rest and transmitted only over HTTPS. Database access requires multi-factor authentication for admin users.

6How long we keep it

Sensitive identity data — passport numbers, passport issue/expiry/place, parent ID numbers, and uploaded passport scans — will be automatically deleted on 14 July 2026, one week after the tour ends. This deletion is scheduled in the database (automated) and audit-logged. We keep non-sensitive trip records (name, role, dietary requirements, medical summary) for two years in case of an insurance claim or follow-up, then delete them.

7Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you or your child;
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong;
  • Withdraw consent and ask us to delete your information (note: if we have already submitted the visa application, we cannot withdraw that submission from the Indian government).

Send any request to alex@rrmelbourne.com.au and we will respond within 14 days.

8Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your information, contact Alex Lewis first. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au, 1300 363 992). The OAIC is free and independent.

9Children

Sixteen of the eighteen travellers on this tour are minors. Information about a minor is collected from the parent or legal guardian. By signing the intake form, the signing parent confirms they have legal authority to provide the information and consent on the child's behalf, and that the other parent (if any) has been informed.
