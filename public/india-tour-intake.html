Privacy

The information on this form is collected to manage the India Tour 2026 and is stored in the RRA Supabase database (Australian-hosted, HTTPS, row-level access control). It is shared only with chaperones on tour, the Indian e-Visa portal, the airline at ticketing, the insurance provider, and treating medical staff in an emergency.

Retention: sensitive identity data (passport numbers, parent IDs) is automatically deleted from our database by 14 July 2026, one week after the tour ends. Non-sensitive trip records are kept for two years for insurance/follow-up.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time — email alex@rrmelbourne.com.au. The full privacy notice (a one-page PDF) is provided alongside this form.
