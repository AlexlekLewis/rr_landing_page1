Royals Way · India Tour 2026

Code of Conduct

26 Jun → 7 Jul 2026 · Jaipur · 18 travellers
This Code of Conduct applies to every player and parent/guardian on the RRA Melbourne India Tour 2026. It is read alongside the Privacy Notice. By ticking the consent box on the intake form, you (the player and, where applicable, the parent or legal guardian) agree to abide by everything below for the duration of the tour. The Code exists to keep every player safe, the team focused, and the program's reputation intact in our host country.

1Supervision & movement

Players are supervised at all times by RRA staff — Alex Lewis (Head Coach) and the CEO of Royals Melbourne. Players must:

  • Stay with the group at all times. Never leave the Jaipur Academy grounds, a hotel, a transport vehicle, or any tour venue without an RRA chaperone.
  • Travel only in transport arranged by the tour. No taxis, rickshaws, or rideshares without a chaperone.
  • Be in their assigned rooms by curfew. Curfew is 10:00 PM IST on training days, 10:30 PM IST on match or cultural days.
  • Inform a chaperone immediately if separated from the group, lost, or unwell.

2Behaviour

RRA Melbourne is a guest of the Rajasthan Royals organisation and of India. Every player represents the academy, Royals Melbourne, and Australian cricket. Players must:

  • Treat hosts, coaches, opposition, officials, hotel staff and the public with respect at all times.
  • Wear team kit at training, matches and Royals events. Smart casual at meals and cultural outings.
  • Engage fully with training sessions, matches, debriefs, and cultural activities — phones away during program time.
  • Use language that is respectful and inclusive. Bullying, slurs, sledging or "banter" that crosses the line is not tolerated.
  • Not bring the program into disrepute on or off the field.

3Alcohol, drugs, smoking & vaping

Zero tolerance. No player may consume or possess alcohol, illegal substances, tobacco, vapes or e-cigarettes at any point during the tour. This applies on and off academy grounds, in transit, and at all hours. Breach results in immediate notification of parents and the consequences in Section 8.

4Phones & social media

  • Phones may be used in down-time for family contact, photos and approved social media.
  • Phones away during training, team meetings, matches, debriefs, and meals as a group.
  • Do not photograph or film Indian Academy staff, opposition players, hotel staff, or the public without their explicit permission.
  • Do not post photos of other players to public social media without the player's (and, for minors, the parent's) consent.
  • No location tags showing the tour group's real-time whereabouts.
  • No public commentary about training plans, match tactics, opposition, or Royals operations.

5Safety, health & medical

  • Disclose any change in your health or any medication needs to a chaperone immediately.
  • Drink only the sealed bottled water provided. Avoid ice from unverified sources and unpeeled fruit from street vendors.
  • Wear sunscreen, a hat, and stay hydrated — temperatures in Jaipur in late June are high.
  • Bring your own personal medication in original packaging with the prescription label.
  • Players authorise Alex Lewis and the Royals Melbourne CEO to act in loco parentis in an emergency, including authorising urgent medical treatment when a parent cannot be reached in time.

6Property & valuables

  • Carry valuables (passport, visa, phone, wallet) on your person or in the room safe at all times.
  • Each player is responsible for their own cricket gear, electronics and personal belongings.
  • Damage to Jaipur Academy facilities, hotels or transport caused by a player is paid for by the player's family.

7Parent / guardian responsibilities

  • Ensure your child has read, understood and signed this Code before departure.
  • Provide accurate medical, dietary and emergency-contact information on the intake form.
  • Be reachable during the tour — Alex Lewis or the CEO will contact you if a serious situation arises.
  • Avoid contacting the team during sessions — channel non-urgent comms through the WhatsApp parent group.
  • Trust the chaperones' decisions on welfare matters in country; we will keep you informed every day.

8Breaches

Most issues are handled by a quiet conversation. For serious or repeated breaches the consequences escalate:

  • Formal warning from the Head Coach, parents notified.
  • Withdrawal from the next training session or match, parents notified.
  • Return home at the family's expense for any breach of Section 3 (alcohol/drugs/tobacco), illegal activity, violence, or any act that compromises another player's safety or the program's reputation. A chaperone or family representative accompanies the player on the journey home.
  • Restitution for any damage caused.
Final decision on a breach rests with the Head Coach in consultation with the CEO of Royals Melbourne. The decision is final for the duration of the tour. Any review takes place after the group returns to Melbourne.

9Photography & media — quick reference

By ticking the Photography & Media consent on the intake form, parent/guardian agrees that RRA and Royals Melbourne may photograph and film tour activities for internal records, social media, press and program promotion. Names are not published alongside individual photos of players without separate consent. Parents may withdraw consent for further use by emailing alex@rrmelbourne.com.au.

10Acknowledgement

By ticking the "I agree to abide by the RRA Melbourne India Tour Code of Conduct" box on the intake form, the player and (for minors) the parent/legal guardian confirm:

  • they have read this Code in full;
  • they understand the consequences of a breach;
  • they will abide by it for the entire duration of the tour, from departure on 25 June 2026 to return to Melbourne after 7 July 2026.
