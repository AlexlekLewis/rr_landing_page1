How purchases are counted

Source: completed Stripe payments for the Elite program, as recorded in the Academy database. "Paid in full" is the $2,995 Autumn 2026 checkout. "Flexi subscription" is the 4-payment option; only its first payment is counted, which is the day the subscription was taken out. "Payment plan or special deal" covers the deposit plus 3 monthly plan, tailored plans, subsidised pricing and an ambassador one-off. Siblings bought separately count as separate purchases. Applications are rows on the Elite application form, which has no program name set.

Locations: each player's suburb from the Elite roster (or their club's suburb where none was given), placed at the suburb's approximate centre. Centres are placed at the centre of Bundoora, Ravenhall and Hallam. Drive times are estimates, not route lookups: road distance is taken as 1.3 times the straight-line distance, with 6 minutes to get going, about 50 km/h for the first 25 km and about 80 km/h after that. That is close to a weekday-evening drive in Melbourne, but real traffic will vary. A family counts against Ravenhall or Hallam only if that centre would be at least 10 minutes closer than Bundoora. Siblings count as two players.
