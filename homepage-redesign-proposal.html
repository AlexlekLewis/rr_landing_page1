Current flow: Hero → About → Video → Programs → Shop → Coaches → FAQ. No features section, no quick-nav, heavier video controls, single-product shop.

Why These Changes

1

New Features section ("The Royals Advantage"). Right now a visitor has to piece together why RRA is worth it from scattered copy. Six clear feature tiles — IPL methodology, all ages, small groups, results, venues, girls' cricket — communicate value in five seconds. This is the single biggest conversion lever.

2

Quick-nav chip bar under the hero. One tap jumps to Programs, Why RRA, Watch, Shop, Coaches or FAQ. Mobile visitors stop endlessly scrolling — they self-select and get where they want faster.

3

Simplified Shop block. Instead of one big featured product, show three compact product tiles + a single "Visit The Shop" button. Feels like a real store preview, less like an ad, and surfaces more range.

4

Cleaner Video block. Strip the custom play/pause/mute cluster down to one obvious play button over a poster frame. Less to think about, fewer things to break.

5

Tighter, benefit-led ordering. Hero → Why RRA (hook) → Programs (action) → Watch (trust) → Shop → Coaches → FAQ. The "why" comes before the "what", so visitors are sold before they browse.

This is a proposal only — no code on the live site has been touched. Tell me what you'd like to change (sections, order, wording, feature tiles) and once it's fully agreed, I'll build it for real.
