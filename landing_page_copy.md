## Program Overview
- **ELITE PROGRAM OVERVIEW**
- A comprehensive, professional environment designed to support the development of a T20 based skillset to help you meet the demands of the modern game.

**CRICKET COACHING & MENTORING**
- **2 on 1:** Players will receive 2 on 1 coaching during particular training sessions to provide for both expert coaching and peer to peer learning, critical for the development of talented players.
- **Squad Training:** Each player will be allocated to a squad that is at a similar age and similar ability based on the assessment of the Academy selection team, and this squad will train together on a regular basis.
- **Specialist (Masterclass):** Coaches and mentors with elite level skills will conduct masterclass sessions. The coaching team will monitor the skill set of the group and determine the appropriate masterclass.
- **Performance Analysis:** Primarily the Rajasthan Royals Academy Management System and Full Track, as well as other tech programs, will form the basis of our monitoring tools.
- **Video Analysis:** Video analysis is a cornerstone of the Elite program, providing you with instant feedback on your development as a player.

**MATCH PLAY / SIMULATION**
- **Matches and Match Simulation:** Players from the Elite Program may be invited to outdoor internal matches and match simulations, staged from time to time through the 2nd half of 2026.
- **Scenario Simulation:** Indoor scenario sessions will be held at Cutting Edge Cricket Centre to provide for assisting players in recognising and navigating their way through specific match situations.
- **Power Hitting Sessions:** The ability to switch into a power hitting mode is a huge part of the modern game and our expert coaches have built a program heavy on building this vital skillset.

**ROYALS OPPORTUNITIES**
- **Rajasthan Royals High Performance Centre:** Each calendar year, talented cricketers from the Elite program may be invited to the Rajasthan Royals High Performance Centre in Nagpur, India.
- **Invitation to Trial with Royals Franchises:** Elite players who show exceptional development and growth may be offered opportunities to trial with Rajasthan Royals, Paarl Royals or Barbados Royals.
- **Visits by Royals Coaches and Scouts:** From time to time, Royals coaches, scouts and franchise players will visit the Elite program to facilitate opportunities for members.

**PROGRAM LENGTH**
- **Intensive Period:** The Elite program is built for a specific purpose and as such is staged in a block of approximately 12-weeks. This high intensity program is a perfect launching pad into the 2026/27 club pre-season and representative trial periods.
- **Beyond the Elite Program:** The performance of players in the Elite program will be continually monitored and assessed during and after their time in the program. Depending on a player's development level and trajectory, the Rajasthan Royals Academy may make further offers to additional future programs.

**ADDITIONAL SERVICES**
- **Individual Development Plans (IDP):** Each player receives a personalised development plan that addresses the whole person: skills, fitness, mindset, recovery, and life.
- **Career Mentorship:** Critical to your development, our expert team will work with you on mapping out cricket career and development opportunities based on your skills and performance.
- **360 Player Development:** Sessions in nutrition, sports & performance psychology, strength & conditioning development and body management.

**APPAREL**
- **Playing Apparel:** Players will receive an apparel pack, consisting of a hat, a training shirt and training shorts.
- **Additional Apparel:** Additional apparel items can be purchased by Academy players, including jumper, track pants, jacket. Only those competing in matches are permitted to purchase playing apparel.

## FAQ (Common Questions)
- Everything you need to know about the Elite Program

**Program Details**
- **What age group is eligible?** Male and female cricketers from age 10/11 through to senior cricketers in their 20's and 30's.
- **When does the program start and how long does it run?** 12 weeks starting early April 2026, with two sessions per week — one weeknight evening and one weekend session.
- **Where are sessions held?** Primarily at Cutting Edge Cricket in Bundoora, Melbourne, with other facilities used periodically.
- **Is it available for female cricketers?** Absolutely. Talent is talent — the Royals back emerging cricketers regardless of gender. There is a specific focus on preparing U15–U18 girls for WBBL, Women's IPL, state, and premier opportunities.
- **Is there a cost involved?** Yes, there is an investment. The program is a premium performance experience with access to elite coaching and resources. Specific costs will be provided to successful applicants.
- **What's included in the program?** Individual Development Plans, video analysis, sports psychology, nutrition, strength & conditioning coaching, official Royals apparel (hat, training shirt, shorts), and performance data reviewed by Royals Group coaches globally.
- **Is it T20 cricket?** Approximately 70-80% T20 skill based, and 20-30% traditional skills. The goal is to ensure players can apply T20 skills to all formats of the game in relevant scenarios.

**Selection & Pathways**
- **Is entry guaranteed?** No. Places are limited and based on merit, skills, and potential.
- **What is the selection process?** Invitation from a RR Academy Melbourne scout → Complete Application → Selection Team Reviews Applications → Possible Trial and Assessment Session → Possible Offer and Acceptance. Even if not selected in the first intake, your details stay on file — the Royals are building something long-term and the Melbourne team will begin touch regarding future programs.
- **What pathway opportunities exist?** Performance-based opportunities include: potential match play against external opposition, invitation to the Royals High Performance Centre in Nagpur, trial opportunities with Rajasthan Royals (IPL), Paarl Royals (SA20), or Barbados Royals (CPL), and visits from franchise coaches and players.
- **Are trials with IPL franchises guaranteed?** No. Trial opportunities are for players demonstrating exceptional skill. However, ALL members are tracked by Royals coaches.
- **Will I go to the High Performance Centre in Nagpur?** Select players will be invited to attend camps at the High Performance facility in Nagpur, India.
- **What if I am at the young end of the age groups?** Younger players will be monitored as future talent. Trial opportunities are likely for older age groups, but younger players benefit from early exposure to Royals systems and data tracking.
- **What happens if I'm not selected in the first intake?** Your details stay on file. The Royals are building a long-term pathway in Melbourne — this is just the first intake.
