## Your Game. Defined.
*6 domains · Nearly 40 individual skill dimensions*

Your Royals Player DNA breaks your game down across six domains — Technical Batting, Technical Bowling, Fielding, Game Intelligence, Physical/Athletic, and Mental/Character — assessed across nearly 40 individual skill dimensions.

This isn't a general report card. It's a precise picture of your game, built from real evidence. Coaches assess you. You assess yourself. The system combines both to build an honest picture that neither guesswork nor gut feel alone can produce.

The guesswork is gone. You know exactly what elite looks like — and exactly where you stand relative to it.

> **Coaches assess you. You assess yourself. The system combines both.**

## Context That Makes Your Score Mean Something.
*Competition Tier · Age Relativity · Real Performance Level*

A score without context is just noise. A 16-year-old performing in Dowling Shield is operating in a completely different environment than a 14-year-old in U14 community cricket.

Your Royals Player DNA accounts for where you play, who you play against, and how old you are relative to your competition — so your assessment reflects your real performance level, not an arbitrary standard.

No inflated confidence. No undeserved discouragement. Just an honest picture of where your game actually sits on the pathway.

> **Where you play. Who you play against. How old you are relative to the field. All accounted for.**

## The Gap That Changes Everything.
*Self-awareness is a trainable skill*

One of the most powerful parts of your Royals Player DNA is the gap between how you see your game and how your coach sees it.

A large gap means there's a conversation worth having — one that can unlock development you didn't know was available to you. A small gap means you have clear self-awareness and you're ready to build on it with purpose.

Learning to see your own game accurately is one of the most trainable skills in cricket. Elite players do it. Your Royals Player DNA helps you start.
