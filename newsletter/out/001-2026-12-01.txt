FROM THE NETS
Training in the heat
Melbourne summer cricket is hot. Drink before you feel thirsty: a few big mouthfuls before training, then small sips at every drinks break. Wear a cap, re-apply sunscreen at the break, and pack a piece of fruit for after. Recovery starts the minute you walk off.
